The server keeps watching this camera and drives the real OS cursor, so you can switch to Chrome or any other window and keep gesturing. The thumb is the mode switch: while it is tucked in, hold your index finger up (other fingers folded) and slide your hand — the cursor slides with it, just like a real trackpad; when you stop, the cursor stops. Push your hand toward the edge of the camera view to sweep a long distance (or reach the taskbar). To scroll, make a peace sign (index + middle spread apart, other fingers down) and slide your hand up or down. To click: stretch your thumb OUT — the cursor freezes and the system knows a click is coming, so it never mistakes a move for a click. Then curl your index fingertip for left-click (fires the moment you curl; two quick curls = double-click) or curl your middle fingertip for right-click. Bring your thumb back in to move again. Open your palm (all fingers up) to release — your real trackpad is always supreme.
